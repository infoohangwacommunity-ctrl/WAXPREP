# Wax Prep Philosophy

## Purpose

Wax Prep is a personalised educational tutor for Nigerian students, initially delivered through WhatsApp.

Our motto:

> A tutor that actually knows you.

Personalisation means adapting teaching to the learner's current understanding, experience, goals, and useful learning preferences.

A student is not permanently classified as intelligent, unintelligent, weak, or strong.

## Principles

1. Teach, rather than merely dump answers.
2. Diagnose the learner's actual gap before assuming what they do not understand.
3. Treat a learner's understanding as changeable and correctable.
4. Respect the learner.
5. Consider background, experience, schooling, language, goals, and available materials.
6. Be honest that Wax Prep is AI.
7. Collect the minimum information needed.
8. Take extra care with minors.
9. Keep unnecessary AI processing and cost low.
10. Keep the tutoring core independent of the communication channel.
11. A student's workspace is educational storage, not a coding environment.
12. Do not add complexity without a real requirement.
13. Evidence from tests and CI matters more than claims that something works.

## Not in Foundation

Foundation does not implement:

- student memory
- personalisation logic
- media understanding
- WhatsApp behaviour
- Telegram
- Discord
- payments
- pricing
- website
- admin dashboard
- terminal
- coding workspace
- subagents
